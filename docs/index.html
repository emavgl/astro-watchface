Galassia

Milky Way Watchface

A minimal analog watch face for Wear OS with a real Milky Way sky behind the dial.

Galassia changes its background every 30 minutes, using a panorama of the night sky split into 32 tiles. The dial includes a date complication, an optional center text complication, and three tick styles.
