Privacy Policy

Galassia: Milky Way Watchface

This policy explains how the Galassia watch face handles information. Galassia is designed to work entirely on your watch and does not collect personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information. Galassia has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Device permissions and network access

  • Galassia does not request Android permissions.
  • Galassia does not access the internet.
  • Your watch face settings, such as the selected tick style, are stored locally by Wear OS on your device. They are not sent to us.

Complications

If you add a date or text complication, Wear OS provides that information to the watch face so it can be displayed on your watch. Galassia does not receive, copy, or transmit complication data.

Third-party services

Galassia is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information related to your use of Google Play under its own privacy policy. Galassia itself does not include third-party services.

Children's privacy

Galassia does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the updated date shown below.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/emavgl/astro-watchface/issues.
